This Policy describes how the Banana King Counterattacks app handles your information. It is short because the app does very little with data: nothing leaves your device.

Summary

Banana King Counterattacks does not collect, transmit or share personal data. The game runs entirely offline. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores on your device

For the game to work, the following is saved locally, in the app's own storage:

  • the name or nickname you type on the start screen;
  • your chosen language;
  • your last score and high score;
  • your current level and sound preferences.

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. It stays on the device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. The name you type is only shown on screen, and you may enter anything you like — we do not ask for your real name.

What the app does NOT do

  • no registration, e-mail, phone number or password;
  • no access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location;
  • no cookies, advertising identifiers or analytics tools;
  • no advertising;
  • no information sent over the internet.

Permissions

The app declares the internet access permission. It is added automatically by the technology used to build the app (Capacitor/WebView) and is not used to send or receive data. All game content — images, fonts and sounds — ships inside the app.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages, including children. Since no data is collected from any user, no data is collected from children either. There is no advertising, no purchases and no communication between players.

Purchases

This version of the app has no active purchases. Should any be added in the future, payments are handled entirely by Google Play under its own privacy policy — we neither receive nor store payment data.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing on our side to access, correct or delete. To erase everything the game has saved, simply uninstall the app or clear its data in your device settings.

Changes

If the app ever handles data differently — for example by adding ads or an online leaderboard — this Policy will be updated before that change takes effect, and the date at the top of this page will change.
